# joshspeagle.com — design & engineering audit

Eight review lenses, all findings verified against source, the built HTML, live screenshots, and (where relevant) a real browser. Verifier corrections are folded in; refuted claims dropped; severities are post-verification.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. The site is well-built and genuinely pre-rendered — no broken pages, no dead links, an idempotent build, and a real design-token system. The problems are concentrated in three places: colour semantics, figure legibility, and hand-maintained duplication.
2. **The colour system has no one-token-one-meaning discipline.** `--violet` *is* `--cat-sla` and `--blue` *is* `--cat-ic`, byte-identical in both themes — so the site's link colour, focus ring and active-chip fill are literally two of the four research-category tokens.
3. Those same four hues are re-spent on authorship roles (Publications), career stages (Mentorship), talk types, teaching departments and service org-types — roughly **six unrelated meanings per hue**, two of which collide on one screen of publications.html.
4. **The palette fails greyscale and colour-vision simulation**: cat-ii vs cat-du differ by 1.8 luminance points, cat-ic vs seg-postdoc by 1.3; under deuteranopia three of four category hues collapse to one lavender. Legends are colour-swatch-only.
5. **Figure type renders at 3.9–6.6 px.** The four inline SVGs use a fixed 820-unit viewBox with 11px text; the two half-width charts are illegible at a 1440px desktop, all four at 390px. This breaks the owner's own canon ("no smaller than body-adjacent") on the page that showcases his dataviz.
6. Three real WCAG failures: `clip-path` on `.btn` clips the focus outline away entirely (2.4.7 AA); 25 homepage opportunity links are distinguished by hue alone at 1.03:1 against surrounding prose (1.4.1 A); desktop nav dropdowns report `aria-expanded` opposite to their actual visibility (4.1.2 A).
7. Publication data has two hard errors: one paper is **double-counted** (137 should be 136), and **31% of papers carry a `researchArea` that contradicts their own category probabilities** — 20 cards show an accent colour matching none of their badges.
8. One copy item is a credibility risk: a News item claims co-authorship on *Konietzka et al. 2024, Nature*. Crossref and arXiv both give a nine-author list that **does not include Speagle**.
9. Structurally, 11 hand-maintained shells duplicate head/nav/footer/theme-script (404 already drifted), the build splices HTML with regex that silently no-ops on a typo'd id, and CI only checks that the build is reproducible — not that it is correct.
10. There is **no unifying visual motif**. Each page's centrepiece uses its own idiom, and the ART logo is a wordmark with sparkles, not the maple-leaf-PCB mark the owner referenced. The Biography timeline (line + ring nodes) and the hero (nodes + edges) are the two cheapest anchors for the trace-and-via language he wants.

## 3. Track A — Visual system & brand (iconography, dataviz)

### A1. Brand/interactive tokens are byte-identical to research-category tokens — High / M
`assets/data/tokens.json:13,20` → `tokens.css:40,47` define `--violet` and `--cat-sla` as the same `#8b7bff` (dark) / `#5b43d6` (light); `tokens.css:44,49` and `:70,75` do the same for `--blue` and `--cat-ic` (`#5aa9ff` / `#1565c0`). `--violet` is used 44× in `redesign.css` — `:focus-visible` outline (`:116`), `.btn-primary` (`:110`), `.chip:hover`/`.chip.is-active` (`:524-528`); `--blue` is the base link colour (`:20`). Evidence: in every publication card a blue "Inference & Computation" badge sits ~20px above the blue ADS/arXiv/DOI links in the identical hue (`scratchpad/v_bluecollide.png`); in the chip row the violet-filled active "All" chip sits beside the violet "Statistical Learning & AI" dot (`v_chiprow.png`).
**Fix:** split the interactive/brand ramp from the category ramp — shift `--violet`/`--blue` in hue or lightness (or shift `--cat-sla`/`--cat-ic`), and never let a chrome colour equal a data colour in either theme.

### A2. The four category hues are re-spent on five more taxonomies — High / L
Same-screen collision: `redesign.css:409` `.seg-primary{fill:#5aa9ff}` == `--cat-ic`, `.seg-student{fill:#ffb454}` == `--cat-du`, `.seg-significant{fill:#34d3e0}` == `--cat-ii`; `:423` repeats them for the RIQ lines. In `crops/publications-dark-desktop-0.png` the "Research mix" bar (orange=Discovery, cyan=Interpretability, blue=Inference) and, ~400px below in the same view, "Publications by year & role" (blue=Primary author, orange=Student-led, cyan=Contributor) are on one screen with three hues byte-identical and contradictory meanings.
Cross-page: `pages_mentorship.py:18-24` binds postdoc→sla, doctoral→ii, masters→ic, bachelors→du (docstring says so explicitly) — verified pixel-identical to the research-mix palette in `scratchpad/v_ment.png`. `redesign.css:322-335` hardcodes the same hexes for Talks types (`.accent-colloquia` #5aa9ff, `.accent-panels` #34d3e0, `.accent-lectures` #a78bff) and Teaching departments; `:257-259` binds Service org-types to the `--cat-*` tokens directly. Light theme diverges partially (`.seg-student` light `#b9760f` ≠ `--cat-du` light `#8f4d00`), so any fix must be specified per theme.
**Fix:** give roles, career stages, talk types, teaching departments and service org-types their own palettes (or one neutral ramp + a second channel), and reserve violet/cyan/blue/amber exclusively for the four research areas everywhere.

### A3. Palette fails greyscale and colour-vision simulation — High / M
Computed relative luminance: cat-ii `#34d3e0` Y=52.7 vs cat-du `#ffb454` Y=54.5 (Δ1.8); cat-ic `#5aa9ff` Y=37.8 vs seg-postdoc `#a99bff` Y=39.1 (Δ1.3); cat-sla Y=26.9 vs seg-other Y=25.2 (Δ1.7). The owner's canon (`ICONOGRAPHY.md:229`) requires ~7.8 for four values. **Light theme is worse**: cat-sla `#5b43d6` Y=11.1 and cat-du `#8f4d00` Y=11.1 are identical; cat-ii vs cat-ic differ by 0.4. Brettel/Viénot simulation: under deuteranopia sla/ii/ic collapse to one lavender (RGB distances 11.2/16.4/21.4); under protanopia sla and ic are 6.0 apart. Because the roles chart's only key is a colour-swatch legend (`build_html.py:671-674`) and the mix bar's key is coloured dots, swatch→mark binding is colour-only. `shots/roles_greyscale.png` confirms a 5-series bar reading as ~2 tones.
**Fix:** run every new token through a luminance/greyscale check before binding it; add a second channel (hatch, marker shape, in-bar direct labels) to the roles/RIQ/mix figures.

### A4. Figure axis/legend type renders at 3.9–6.6 px — High / M
All four SVGs use `viewBox="0 0 820 …"` (`build_html.py:640,694,745,806`) with `.pf-svg{width:100%}` and `.pf-axis`/`.pf-legend{font-size:11px}` (`redesign.css:400-403`) and no responsive override. Measured in Chromium: at 1440px the two half-width charts (`.pub-figures` is a 2-col grid, `redesign.css:395`) render 490px wide → scale 0.598 → **6.6px**; at 1024px → 5.4px; at 768px → 4.0px; at 390px the SVG box is 292px → scale 0.356 → **3.9px**. Visible in `crops/publications-dark-desktop-0.png` and `publications-dark-mobile-0.png`. Site `--fs-label` is 12.48px, so 11px undercuts body-adjacent even before scaling.
**Fix:** stop scaling label text with the viewBox — move legends to HTML siblings of the SVG (as the figcaption already is), and/or give the grid-placed charts a narrower viewBox and set axis type in non-scaling units. This is the single highest-value dataviz fix.

### A5. Role tags sit beside category badges in the same hue — Medium / S
`redesign.css:598-609` `.tag.stu` `#ff9d4d` vs `.b-du` `--cat-du` `#ffb454`; `.tag.pd` `#a99bff` vs `.b-sla` `#8b7bff`. `.badge` and `.tag` share byte-identical geometry (`:569-577` vs `:583-591`), so shape gives the reader nothing. In light theme they are near-identical: `.tag.stu` `#8a4500` vs cat-du `#8f4d00`; `.tag.pd` `#4733c4` vs cat-sla `#5b43d6`. Verified on "It's a matter of time…" (amber Student-led beside amber Discovery & Understanding) and "Disk Wind Feedback…" (violet Postdoc-led beside violet Statistical Learning & AI), `crops/publications-dark-desktop-1.png`.
**Fix:** move role tags to the neutral treatment `.tag.feat` already uses (`redesign.css:594-598`) — outline + `--text-2` + an icon or initial.

### A6. No unifying motif; PCB/trace opportunity unclaimed — Medium / L
`grep -rniE 'circuit|pcb|trace|via-|solder|maple'` across CSS/JS/SVG returns **zero hits**. The four page centrepieces are four unrelated idioms: canvas particle network (`hero.js`), hand-rolled inline SVG charts (`build_html.py:619-830`), DOM stage bars (`pages_mentorship.py:166-203`), a plain 1px rule with ring nodes (`biography.html`). Section chrome is generic: `.section{border-top:1px solid var(--border)}` (`:137`) is the only divider. The cross-page signature is a single `✦` glyph per shell. `assets/images/art-logo.svg` and `art-logo-mod.svg` are an "ART" wordmark with a diagonal slash and four-point sparkles — **not** the maple-leaf-PCB mark from the brief.
**Opportunity (not designed here):** the Biography timeline is already a line with ring vias, and the hero is already nodes-and-edges. Extending one trace-and-via language to section dividers, chart axes/grids, card corner treatments and the ART logo would convert two coincidences into a system.

### A7. The `✦` sparkle mark is the current AI-product cliché — Medium / M
`scripts/generate_favicons.py` `star_points()` fills a 4-point violet star for `favicon-16/32` and `apple-touch-icon.png` (rendered: `scratchpad/v_icon.png`); `favicon.svg` repeats it with a gradient; `make_og_card.py:62` places it twice on the OG card; every shell's nav brand carries the literal `✦` (`index.html:38`); and `redesign.css:216` `.highlight-box h3::before{content:"\2726"}` prefixes "On Mentorship" / "Teaching Philosophy". It carries the whole identity and is near-identical to the Gemini/Copilot/Notion-AI glyph.
**Fix:** restyle rather than retire — a 4-point star also reads as a diffraction spike, which is on-theme. A via/trace junction that still reads as a star ties the mark to the PCB motif in A6.

### A8. ART-panel emoji icons don't theme-adapt — Medium / S
`index.html:95` renders `<span class="art-hl-icon">🔬</span>` and `👥` from `content.json:109,114`. Pixel-identical across themes (`v_emoji_light.png` shows a blue-and-white microscope on cream), unlike the four research-area icons at `index.html:120` which recolor via `redesign.css:148` `.research-card.sla .icon{color:var(--cat-sla)}`. A microscope is also the wrong metaphor for astrostatistics.
**Fix:** replace both with the same stroke-SVG vocabulary as the research cards; edit the `icon` field in `content.json`, not just markup.

### A9. Citations chart is drawn in `--violet` directly under "violet = Statistical Learning" — Medium / M
`redesign.css:414-416` `.pf-area`/`.pf-cit-line`/`.pf-dot` all use `var(--violet)`, and `build_html.py:908-909` emits the Research-mix figure immediately followed by the citations figure. `--grad-text` (`tokens.css:46`) runs violet→blue→cyan (sla→ic→ii) and paints every KPI number on the site (`redesign.css:201,671`), regardless of what is counted.
**Fix:** draw single-series magnitude charts in a neutral/reference ink; stop routing KPI numbers through a gradient built from category tokens.

### A10. RIQ legend swatch is undefined CSS → renders default black — Medium / S
`build_html.py:772` emits `<rect class="pf-sw seg-all">`; neither `.pf-sw` nor `.seg-all` exists in `redesign.css`, so the "All papers (482)" swatch falls back to SVG default black — invisible on dark, wrong on light. The series itself is a grey dashed `.ln-all` (`redesign.css:422`).
**Fix:** define `.seg-all` to match `.ln-all`, and add `.pf-sw` sizing alongside the other swatch rules.

### A11. Publications carries an unusually large concurrent colour budget — Low / M
Four category hues + amber Student-led + violet Postdoc-led + four role-chart hues + the violet-tinted active chip on one page. (Corrections to the original claim: `.tag.feat` is already neutral, links are blue not violet, and there is no `btn-primary` on this page.)
**Fix:** resolving A2 and A5 removes most of it; no separate work item.

### A12. Mix-bar labels are a detached legend, not direct labels — Low / S
`build_html.py:807-822`: segments are laid out by cumulative x, then a second loop restarts at `lx=bx0` and flows labels by string length. Measured on `shots/mix2_dark_grey.png`: "Interpretability 21%" (x≈345–520) sits entirely inside the Discovery segment (x≈192–664); "Statistical Learning 14%" sits at x≈697–905 while its segment is at x≈1105–1242. In greyscale the four legend dots are indistinguishable, so nothing binds label to segment.
**Fix:** print label+percentage inside any segment above ~10% (three of four fit), keep the key only for slivers.

### A13. Mix-bar percentages sum to 101% — Low / S
`build_html.py:818-820` rounds each segment independently. `publications.html` ships "Discovery 45% · Interpretability 21% · Inference 21% · Statistical Learning 14%". The same un-corrected value goes into each segment's `aria-label` (`:812`), so screen-reader users hear 101% too.
**Fix:** largest-remainder (Hamilton) rounding in one helper used by both call sites.

### A14. RIQ "typical range" band bounds are never printed — Low / S
The band is `yv(150)..yv(60)` (`build_html.py:746-749`), 14% of plot height and clearly visible with its label inside it — the original "thin sliver" claim is overstated, and the top series is 3.1×, not 8×, the band. What is missing is the numbers: the level the whole argument is read against is unnamed.
**Fix:** print `60–150` on the band or axis; consider a panel-header measurement ("~3× typical by 2025").

### A15. Mentee current-vs-former is opacity-only — Low / S
`pages_mentorship.py:178-181` emits `mc-{color}` for both, differing only by `mc-faded`; `redesign.css:229-230` sets `opacity:.38` on a 16px track. Worst real case is Undergraduate (1 current / 56 former) where "current" is a ~2px amber sliver. The legend swatches are neutral grey (`:225`), so the legend never demonstrates the solid/faded pairing.
**Fix:** hatch or outline-vs-solid for "former"; hue-match the legend swatches.

### A16. Awards ships full list chrome for 4 items — Low / S
`awards.html` has a search input, a "Newest first" select, and a single `All 4` chip that can never filter anything, from `pages_shared.scaffold()`.
**Fix:** thresholds in `scaffold()` — omit search/sort below ~10 items, omit the chip row when only `all` exists. Generic, not an Awards special case.

### A17. Light-theme hero backdrop has no light override — Low / M
`hero.js:18-21` does define an independently tuned `PAL.light`, and `redesign.css:92-94` overrides `.hero .dawn` — but `.hero .backdrop` (`:77-80`) and `.hero .vignette` (`:83-85`) have no light variant, and the vignette is `radial-gradient(… var(--bg-0) …)` where `--bg-0` flips `#06080f`→`#f6f6f1`. The scrim that focuses the dark composition becomes a near-white blob under the violet/cyan wash — the lavender-peach smear.
**Fix:** author a light-theme backdrop/vignette rather than inheriting the dark one.

### A18. Hero is faint on mobile — Low / M
`redesign.css:124-133` sets `.hero canvas{opacity:.5}` and replaces the left-anchored vignette with a near-uniform veil. Measured at 390px: the violet/cyan backdrop reads (11,15,26) vs (39,60,93) at the equivalent desktop point (~4× drop); the dawn is squeezed into the leftmost ~25%. A deliberate readability tradeoff, but most visitors never see the composition.
**Fix:** a mobile-specific treatment (denser/brighter sample, or a static SVG variant) rather than a passive dim. See also D11 — the mobile canvas is also geometrically distorted.

### A19. The hero is an unlabelled figure — Low / S
`hero.js:1-5` and `sampleTarget()` (`:32-39`) encode a real SBI narrative — data → network activation → a rotated correlated Gaussian posterior — in 221 lines. `index.html:64` is `<canvas aria-hidden="true">` with no caption. (`aria-hidden` is correct for decoration; this is an unrealised opportunity, not an a11y defect.)
**Fix:** one kicker line binding the animation to the tagline's own vocabulary, converting invisible narrative into the site's strongest figure.

### A20. Personality is confined to a handful of elements — Low / M
`content.json:1883` ("A Brief History of `<s>`Time`</s>` MCMC"), the 404 "Lost in parameter space" copy, the Kumo/Aki snapshots, the ART logotype, the RIQ metric, and the first-person "Why these figures?" note are the non-templatable moments. The seven secondary pages otherwise share one `scaffold()` layout.
**Fix:** extend the voice deliberately (page taglines, Biography photos, small hand-drawn touches) rather than adding decoration.

### A21. `--cat-ii` / `--cat-ic` badge contrast has no headroom in light theme — Low / S
`--cat-ii #0a7280` on its own 14% tint (`redesign.css:579`) = **4.56:1**; `--cat-ic #1565c0` = **4.67:1**. Both pass AA for 12.5px/600 text, with almost no margin (siblings measure 5.22–5.24 on the same basis).
**Fix:** darken both slightly (e.g. cat-ii → ~`#086670`) to match the siblings' margin.

## 4. Track B — Copy & content

### B1. News item claims co-authorship on a *Nature* paper he did not co-author — High / S
`content.json` `sections.news.items` "Feb 2024": "Co-author on the finding that the galaxy's largest gaseous structure oscillates (Konietzka et al., Nature)". arXiv and Crossref (DOI `10.1038/s41586-024-07127-3`) both give nine authors — Konietzka, Goodman, Zucker, Burkert, Alves, Foley, Swiggum, Koller, Miret-Roig. **Speagle is not among them.** He *is* an author on the 2020 Alves et al. discovery paper, which is in the publication list.
**Fix:** rewrite the blurb to drop "Co-author on" (frame it as press coverage of a line of work he contributed to), and audit the other press items for the same overclaim pattern.

### B2. Publications meta description is stale on two of three numbers — Medium / S
`publications.html:7` advertises "130 papers, 16,908 citations, h-index 44". `publications_data.json` metrics: **137 papers, 18,503 citations**, h-index 44. The page body itself renders 137 and 18,503. No generator emits `meta name="description"` anywhere — all 11 are hand-typed, which is why they drifted.
**Fix:** generate description/og:description in `build_html.py` from `content.json` + `publications_data.json` so numbers can never go stale. (Also resolves B3.)

### B3. Five meta descriptions exceed ~160 chars; 404 has none — Medium / S
Measured: index 193, mentorship 177, service 175, talks 174, teaching 171. Under 160: publications 153, awards 154, software 151, news 135, biography 126. `404.html` has no description at all.
**Fix:** trim to ≤160 as part of B2's generator; add one to 404.

### B4. Talks / Awards / Teaching / Service cards are inert — Medium / M
`grep -n "href=" scripts/pages_{talks,awards,service,teaching}.py` returns nothing. Generated-region link counts: talks 0, awards 0, teaching 0, service 1 (in prose). `content.json` `sections.talks.categories[].talks[]` carries only title/event/location/date/year/type — no `url` field, so the gap is structural. Sharpest instance: `sections.teaching.resources` is titled "Educational Resources" and says "I develop and maintain several open educational resources including course materials, tutorials, and software packages" — and renders with zero links.
**Fix:** add a `url`/`links` field where a natural target exists and render it as a card CTA, matching Software/News/Publications. Start with the Educational Resources block.

### B5. Footer has no contact or social links — Medium / S
All 11 shells carry the identical two spans ("© 2026 Joshua S. Speagle · 沈佳士" / "Astronomy · Statistics · AI · Updated August 2026") with no `<a>` of any kind. `grep -o 'mailto:[^"]*' *.html` matches **index.html only**; the ORCID, Scholar and ADS links likewise. ### B6. Page taglines are generic filler — Low / S
`content.json:3388` publications: "Peer-reviewed research and scholarly contributions." — rendered directly above tiles reading 137/14/10/17/25. `:3408` awards: "Recognition and achievements in academia and research." — whose first word repeats the kicker "Recognition" verbatim, above exactly four named honors.

### B7. Homepage headings mix register and casing — Low / S

Across the four homepage H2s (`index.html:92,105,118,129`): one interrogative among three declaratives, and sentence case ("Statistics, AI & astronomy") beside title case ("Collaboration & Mentorship", "The Astrostatistics Research Team").

### B13. Spelling inconsistencies — Low / S

## 7. Proposed target architecture (build)

**Goal: one templated shell, derived dates, checks that can fail.**

1. **`render_shell(page_key, header_html, content_html, meta)`** in `build_html.py`, driven by a `PAGES` registry (id, nav label, nav group, canonical path) plus `content.json`'s `pages.<page>`. It emits head, nav, footer, theme-init script and the header band. Deletes 11 duplicated copies of each, makes `aria-current`, canonical URLs and OG tags correct by construction, and closes the 404-drift class of bug (D16) at source. Home gets a `pages.home` entry so its kickers stop living in Python (C2).
2. **Retire the regex splice.** Either `<!-- BUILD:START id=… -->` sentinels matched non-greedily, or `html.parser.HTMLParser` splicing by node position. Either way, **assert exactly one match per container and raise** — today a typo'd id is a silent no-op that CI cannot see (C1). Wrap each page's `build_page()` in its own try/except so one bad data file can't block the other nine (C14).
3. **Derived, never hand-typed:**
   - meta/OG descriptions generated from `content.json` + `publications_data.json` (kills B2/B3 permanently);
   - footer "Updated <Month Year>" and `sitemap.xml` `lastmod` derived from git/build time, not hand-maintained;
   - `software_data.json` / `publications_data.json` `lastUpdated` owned exclusively by their fetch scripts (C16);
   - `researchArea` computed as argmax at pipeline time (D5); role counts sourced from one place (C9).
4. **One escaping module.** All six variants collapse into `pages_shared.py`; `build_html.py` imports rather than redefines (C6), and every `href` goes through `url_attr` (C7).
5. **Declared, not derived, accents.** Categories/departments declare an `accent` key in `content.json` (as software groups already do) instead of slugifying free text into CSS class names (C3). This also forces every new taxonomy to pick a token explicitly — the structural fix behind A2.
6. **CI that can fail on correctness**, invoked as `npm run build` so `package.json` is the single source of build order (C11):
   - the existing staleness diff;
   - **contract check**: every `[data-lv-item]` under a listview offering a `year` sort carries `data-year` (already violated — C5);
   - **container check**: every `#<page>-content`/`#<page>-header` was actually substituted;
   - **token lint**: no raw hex in `redesign.css` outside `var(--…)`; no undefined custom property (would have caught `--fs-sm`, `.pf-sw`/`.seg-all`);
   - **palette check**: pairwise luminance ≥ ~7.8 across any set of ≥4 category tokens, run over `tokens.json` (A3);
   - HTML well-formedness + internal link check (both pass today — cheap regression guards);
   - pytest over the pure helpers once date parsing is consolidated into `pages_shared.py` (C13);
   - external link check on a schedule, not per-push.
7. **One list widget.** Grouped mode in `listview.js`; `publications.js` and `mentorgroups.js` deleted; mentorship migrated to `data-lv-*`, which delivers its missing filters for free (C10 + D19).
