Teaching 
Undergraduate and graduate courses in astronomy and statistics, plus short courses and workshops on Bayesian methods and computing. 
6 Courses 
11 Offerings 
6 Years active 
Teaching Philosophy 
I learn best by doing, and I teach that way too: most of my courses are built around working through real problems with real data. What I most want students to walk away with is the confidence to think critically about a method and to explain clearly what it does.

On Mentorship 
Mentorship is the part of my work I care about most. I try to keep the group collaborative and genuinely inclusive — a place where people from different backgrounds can settle in, find the questions that interest them across AI, statistics, data science, and astrophysics, and get real support while they chase them.

About 
Biography 
A short career timeline. 
1990s–2011 
Childhood 
USA & China 
Shuttled between the US (around Milwaukee) and China (Beijing and Shanghai). Fell in love with astronomy at age 17 after reading From Eternity to Here by Sean Carroll . 
2011–2015 
Undergraduate 
Boston, Massachusetts 
Bachelor's Degree with Honors in Astrophysics and Physics at Harvard University . Worked on a handful of research projects across astronomy along the way. 
2015–2016 
Project Academic Support Staff 
Kashiwa, Japan 
Worked with Alexie Leauthaud and Kevin Bundy at Kavli IPMU . Began shifting my focus to astrostatistics and data science. 
2016–2020 
Graduate School 
Boston, Massachusetts 
Master's and Doctoral Degrees at Harvard . Thesis: "Mapping the Milky Way in the Age of Gaia" under Doug Finkbeiner , Charlie Conroy , and Daniel Eisenstein , with additional supervision by Alyssa Goodman . 
2020–2022 
Postdoctoral Fellow 
Toronto, Canada 
Banting Fellow and Dunlap Fellow at University of Toronto , working with Gwen Eadie . 
2022–Present 
Assistant Professor Now 
Toronto, Canada 
Joint appointment in Astronomy & Astrophysics and Statistical Sciences. Co-leading the Astrostatistics Research Team (ART) with Gwen Eadie. 
Jan–Apr 2025 
Research Scientist Concurrent 
Toronto, Canada (Remote) 
While on leave from U of T, worked as a Research Scientist at River Run Trading on quantitative trading strategies for crypto assets.
