Mark · pins · vias · pads

The asterism mark from B (a trace that ends in a star) in the energy colour. On the board: a hollow ring is a via (junction), a filled square is a pin (where a trace enters a chip), a filled disc is a category pad.

Chips as cards

Every list card is a component: a raised surface with square-ish corners, a pin-1 notch, a mono metadata line (year · venue · role), a serif title, and pads for categories. A trace arrives at its left pin; it lights when the card enters the viewport.
